Lot of rumors, lot of ideas, conspiracies surround the 3i/Atlas. I am here to state what is known, what happened, what was released to public domain, I wont shed any of my personal views on the whole situation. Rest is up to the reader to understand and decipher what the texts could mean.

### Detection and Early History

On July 1st Asteroid Terrestrial-impact Last Alert System (ATLAS) telescope in Río Hurtado, Chile, located 3i/Atlas on the border of the constellations Serpens Cauda and Sagittarius, 4.51 AU from the Sun and 3.50 AU from Earth. For context the average distance of earth from sun is 1 AU, so it was well inside Jupiter's orbit (5.2 AU). The IAU Minor Planet Center added 3i/Atlas to NEO confirmation list on 1st July soon it appeared on NASA/JPL website (https://cneos.jpl.nasa.gov/scout/#/object/A11pl3Z).
