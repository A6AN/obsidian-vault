# Lessons

### Lesson 1: Rich don't work for money

1. One needs to give up fear and greed. Fear of losing money, the fear keeps us into the trap of needing money, for paying bills of buying toys, etc. They start seeking financial stability, and seek security which stops them from moving forward. One needs to give up this fear, take calculated risks break the financial depended cycle and start managing risks to grow further.
2. Greed or the desire for quick money can make one take impulsive risks which generally end up in the wrong way. Risks such as gambling, over-leveraging or even taking up a job can pull you back in the cycle and hinder your growth. Instead of being greedy focus on long term financial education, investment and creating passive sources of money.
