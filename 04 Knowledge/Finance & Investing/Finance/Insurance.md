# Part 2 - Insurance

What is term/life insurance? 


A set of money that pays to you in case of death or after a certain period of time.

Never mix your insurance and your investment because this way you will neither get a decent investment nor a decent insurance. 

Insurance is an expense and that’s about it.

  

What is Health insurance?

Medical inflation in India is as high as 15%. Health insurance pays for your medical bills. Buying health insurance at a young age helps in lower premiums.

  

Tips:

1. When buying term insurance, the company does medical tests. Based on these tests, your premium comes out. Smokers/Drinkers have a bigger term insurance.  
2. Critical Illness rider is a must as it helps not only during your illness, but also helps pay for the lost income during the critical illness.
3. Don’t fall for the trap of buying a term plan till you are 99 years old. 
4. “In most industries, the existence of middlemen is generally frowned upon. However, the insurance industry is an exception in that regard. Insurance companies generally reject a claim even if they have the slightest suspicion, and then they expect the customers to seek further recourse.”
5. Do not lie on the proposal form.
6. “It could be very tempting to buy a fully decked-out policy with amazing features at attractive premiums. However, doing this without fully understanding the insurer's profile is dangerous. Features like unlimited restoration and 100% bonus matter little if the insurer has no track record or has a reputation for denying claims.”
7. Check for Zonal co-payment.
8. Check for specific illnesses, the illnesses that aren’t paid.
