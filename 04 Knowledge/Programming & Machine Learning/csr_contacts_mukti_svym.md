## Outreach Strategy for Mukti / SVYM

> [!TIP]
> **Proven Email Template Structure:**
> 1. **Subject:** "[SVYM/Mukti] CSR Partnership Proposal — [Company Name] × [Your programme area]"
> 2. **Para 1:** One line about the NGO + one impressive impact stat
> 3. **Para 2:** Specific alignment with their CSR focus area (research this per company above)
> 4. **Para 3:** Specific ask (grant / implementation partner / employee volunteering)
> 5. **Attachments:** 80G certificate, FCRA cert (if applicable), 1-page brochure

> [!NOTE]
> **Priority Tier 1** (most accessible, active grant-making):
> Wipro Foundation · Tech Mahindra Foundation · ICICI Foundation · Kotak Education Foundation · HDB Financial Services · Titan Company
>
> **Priority Tier 2** (structured process, worth pursuing):
> Tata Steel Foundation · Capgemini · LTTS · Siemens Healthineers · Texas Instruments
>
> **Priority Tier 3** (hard to crack / special process):
> Oak Foundation (LOE only) · KPMG Foundation (invitation-based) · Canara Bank (bureaucratic)
