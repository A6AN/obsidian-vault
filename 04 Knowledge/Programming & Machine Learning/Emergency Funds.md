# Part 1 - Emergency Funds

Emergencies are something we can’t control, but what we can control is our preparedness to fight such emergencies. 

Emergency funds are saved for emergencies so you don’t have to scramble for money at the last moment.

Step 1: Estimating the amount of emergency corpus you need. The right amount of money is the one that lets you sleep peacefully at night.

Step 2: Collecting the fund. Collecting the emergency corpus can be a process that can be carried out over months, like an SIP. Make sure not to invest your emergency fund in the stock market or mutual funds as they introduce volatility and can prove to be a risky step during emergencies. Instead, [[Fixed Deposits]], [[Liquid Funds]], [[Arbitrage Funds 1]] and short-term bonds can be a great idea.

An emergency fund is quick access to funds with capital protection; any financial product that doesn’t meet this definition isn’t the right place to park your corpus.

Step 3: Never use the emergency corpus in situations other than emergencies; be sure to remember why you made it in the first place.
