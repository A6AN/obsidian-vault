# Generalization - Jack of all Trades

Specialists are people who are specialist in a specific field, someone who knows a lot about a certain field and excels in it. Example: Nuclear Engineers.

Generalist are people who have a wide range of knowledge in many fields and hold many skills, applying their knowledge to a field or variety of fields.

##### Why Generalists?
Generalists are needed to lead specialists, pulling their strings and making society run with order.
Generalist need to be at least above average in their fields to effectively manage and lead specialists.


##### Craftsmen vs Cogs 
Generalists are the "craftsmen" who leverage specialists as the "cogs" in the machine, focusing on outcome-based thinking to maximize output.

***OBT - Outcome Based Thinking*** is based on getting the maximum output rather than optimizing the input or the individual tasks.

The basic idea is that a generalist (craftsman) can use a team of specialists (cogs) to get a better, far superior output compared to a single specialist working alone.

In the rapidly evolving technological landscape, specialization alone is no longer sufficient, as many roles and jobs are susceptible to automation and disruptive innovation.

To avoid being replaced, individuals need to become generalists, capable of learning and adapting to new skills and challenges.
